# Campus Crypt — Floor Themes & Title March (original, v3)

These tracks are an **original composition** written and generated for the
Campus Crypt game. They develop the same original Fight March motif used in
the earlier 8-bit sketch (a falling major 3rd, a leap of a 5th, then a
stepwise descent), recast in a 32-bit-era orchestral-synth JRPG style.

They are **not** a quotation, paraphrase, arrangement, or derivation of the
Kennesaw State University fight song (James Sochinski, 2012), nor of any
other existing fight song, march, or game soundtrack. Melody, harmony, and
drum patterns were composed specifically for this project.

v3 is a remaster of the v2 arrangements. Compositions, tempos, keys, forms,
and loop lengths are unchanged. The mix is louder and clearer: brighter
instrument voices, shorter and drier hall reverb, an 80 Hz high-pass on the
pads, a gentle +2.5 dB high-shelf around 3.5 kHz, integrated-loudness
matching, a −1 dBTP true-peak limiter, and a higher Vorbis quality. The v1
8-bit sketch and the v2 folder are left untouched.

- Style: 32-bit-era console RPG (PS1 / Saturn / SNES-orchestral)
- Voices: FM/additive brass, layered strings and pads, horn countermelody,
  harp and pizzicato, timpani, snare, cymbal swells, bass; stereo with hall reverb
- Form: ten seamless floor loops that escalate in tempo, density, harmony,
  and urgency, plus a heroic title-screen march
- Tempo: 100 BPM (Floor 1) rising to 150 BPM (Floor 10); title march at 128 BPM
- Keys: D major → D dorian → B minor → modulating rise → E major (key lift)
- Format: 32 kHz, stereo, Vorbis (.ogg)
- Loudness: about −16 to −15 LUFS on floors 1–5, about −14 LUFS on the title
  and floors 6–10; true-peak ceiling −1 dBTP, no clipping
- License: [CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
  Original work for Campus Crypt, dedicated to the public domain.
