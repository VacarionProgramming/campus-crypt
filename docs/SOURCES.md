# Campus Crypt — Sources

These pages informed the warning signs and safer next moves in the encounters. The game teaches players to notice warning signs and name a better next step. It does not claim to prevent scams or improve grades.

- [FTC, How To Recognize and Avoid Phishing Scams](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/how-recognize-avoid-phishing-scams) — Informs **The Inbox Mimic** (unexpected urgent messages, look-alike sites, and attempts to steal passwords or other personal information).
- [FTC, Job Scams](https://consumer.ftc.gov/articles/job-scams) — Informs **The Internship Impostor** (too-good offers, requests to deposit a check and send money back, and paying or sharing financial details to “get hired”).
- [FTC, ReportFraud.ftc.gov](https://reportfraud.ftc.gov/) — Informs the safer next move in **The Internship Impostor** (where to report a suspected job or fake-check scam).
- [FBI Internet Crime Complaint Center (IC3)](https://www.ic3.gov/) — Informs **The Internship Impostor** (reporting cyber-enabled crime such as fake employment schemes).
- [Federal Student Aid, How To Avoid Student Loan Scams](https://studentaid.gov/articles/scams/) — Informs **The Inbox Mimic** (Federal Student Aid and its partners will never ask for a StudentAid.gov username or password; official email comes only from specific `studentaid.gov` addresses; do not use a link from an unexpected message—go directly to StudentAid.gov).
- [KSU UITS, IT Security](https://campus.kennesaw.edu/offices-services/uits/security/index.php) — Campus starting point for KSU IT security guidance used across the digital-safety rooms.
- [KSU UITS, Phish Market](https://campus.kennesaw.edu/offices-services/uits/security/phish-market/index.php) — Informs **The Inbox Mimic** (check for a non-`@kennesaw.edu` sender, beware urgency, hover don’t click, and report with Phish Alert or `abuse@kennesaw.edu`).
- [KSU UITS, Security Tips](https://campus.kennesaw.edu/offices-services/uits/security/security-tips.php) — Informs **The Password Hydra** (unique passwords, at least 12 characters, never share a KSU password; UITS will never ask for it) and **The Internship Impostor** (watch for faux job offers that require payment up front).

**The Deadline Wraith** is a planning lesson (prioritize, break work into steps, don’t postpone the whole pile). It is not drawn from a scam-agency page.
